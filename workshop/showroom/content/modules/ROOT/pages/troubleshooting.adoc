= Troubleshooting
:navtitle: Troubleshooting

Most problems in this lab have one of a few causes. Check these first, then ask
the facilitator.

== Before Module 4

* **BFF or proxy shows 503, `Client not found`, or an error page.** Expected
  until your realm, its clients, and Module 4 are done.
* **Create realm is missing.** Your account lacks the delegated
  `create-realm` permission. Ask the facilitator; do not change roles in the
  `master` realm.
* **A user gets an extra profile form at first sign-in.** First name, last
  name, or email is missing on the user.

== Sign-in problems

* **`Invalid parameter: redirect_uri`.** Compare the client's redirect URI
  with the app's callback address: scheme, host, path, trailing slash.
* **Signed in as the wrong user.** The apps share one Keycloak session. Sign out
  of every app you used, then sign in again.
* **The proxy loops back to Keycloak.** Check the `proxy-audience` mapper
  (Module 3) and the `client-secret` key in `proxy-secrets`.

== Application problems

* **A pod restarts after you change configuration.** Read its log in
  **Workloads → Pods →** _pod_ **→ Logs**, or search **Observe → Logs**. A
  wrong issuer stops the BFF at startup (Module 4).
* **Changes have no effect.** Pods read ConfigMaps and Secrets at startup;
  restart the rollout.
* **Every request returns 401.** Check the issuer character by character, and
  the `orders-api-aud` scope on the client you signed in with.
* **Manager writes return 502.** Expected until Module 6. After Module 6,
  follow the trace and search the logs for `/inventory/reserve` and `/notify`.
* **`ImagePullBackOff`.** A reservation problem, not a lab task; tell the
  facilitator.

== Logs and traces

* Results can take up to a minute to appear. If **Run Query** shows old
  results, click it again or use the refresh button.
* **Traces page shows no Tempo instance or no permission.** Select Tempo
  instance `tracing / workshop`, then Tenant `{attendee_id}`. Other tenant names
  are listed but only yours opens.
* **Observe → Logs or Traces is missing.** Ask the facilitator to check the
  logging and tracing setup.

== Ground rules

Work only in your realm `{realm_name}` and namespace `{attendee_namespace}`.
Never paste passwords, client secrets, tokens, or cookies into chat, tickets,
or screenshots. If an app URL or realm belongs to someone else, stop and tell
the facilitator.
