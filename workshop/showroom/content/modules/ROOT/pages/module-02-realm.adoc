= Module 2: Create your realm and users
:navtitle: 2. Create a realm

== Know

The Keycloak `create-realm` permission permits a delegated user to create a
realm and administer the realm it created. It does not require the global
`admin` role. A realm is a separate identity boundary inside the shared
Keycloak service.

== Do

. Open the
  link:{keycloak_origin}/admin/master/console/[Keycloak administration console,window=_blank]
  and sign in with your username and password from xref:details.adoc[]. This
  account lives in the Keycloak `master` realm; keep its password unchanged.
. In the left menu choose **Manage realms**, then **Create realm**. Enter the realm name `{realm_name}`, keep **Enabled** on,
  and click **Create**.
+
image::m02-create-realm.png[Create realm form]

. Keycloak switches to your new realm. Open **Realm settings** and, next to
  **Endpoints**, click **OpenID Endpoint Configuration**. Confirm that
  `issuer` is exactly `pass:a[{issuer}]`.
. Open **Users → Create new user** (or **Add user**). Enter username `reader`,
  email `reader@example.invalid`, first name `Reader`, and last name `Lab`, then
  click **Create**. Keycloak requires the first and last name; without them
  the user sees an extra profile form at first sign-in.
+
image::m02-create-user.png[Create user form for reader]

. On the user's **Credentials** tab, click **Set password**. Enter a password
  you choose (not your attendee password), turn **Temporary** **Off**, and
  click **Save**, then confirm **Save password** in the dialog that follows.
+
image::m02-set-password.png[Set password dialog with Temporary turned off]

. Repeat the last two steps for `manager` (`manager@example.invalid`, first
  name `Manager`, last name `Lab`). Write down both lab passwords.

Leave both users without realm-role assignments for now. In Module 3, create
the `user` and `manager` roles, make `manager` include `user`, then assign the
roles to these users. Keycloak cannot assign a role before it exists.

== Check

Confirm both users appear under **Users**, are enabled, and have the expected
synthetic email, and that realm administration works inside your realm. The proxy exercise accepts these
unverified synthetic email claims. The role check happens after the roles are
created in Module 3.

**Expected:** your issuer is `pass:a[{keycloak_origin}/realms/{realm_name}]` and you
cannot administer another attendee's realm or change server-wide settings.

== If something is wrong

If **Create realm** is missing, stop and ask the facilitator to check your
delegated `create-realm` permission. If the realm name collides, do not rename
another attendee's realm; request your exact assigned name. Do not grant
yourself `admin` or add roles in the master realm.
