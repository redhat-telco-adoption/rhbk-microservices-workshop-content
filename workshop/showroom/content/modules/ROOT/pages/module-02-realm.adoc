= Module 2: Create your realm and users
:navtitle: 2. Create a realm

== Know

The Keycloak `create-realm` permission permits a delegated user to create a
realm and administer the realm it created. It does not require the global
`admin` role. A realm is a separate identity boundary inside the shared
Keycloak service.

== Do

. Open the link:{keycloak_origin}/admin/master/console/[Keycloak administration console] and sign in using your allocated
  facilitator-provided account.
. This account authenticates in the Keycloak `master` realm. Do not use
  OpenShift Console SSO for workshop realm administration; it is a separate
  login flow.
. In the master realm, choose **Create realm**.
. Use the exact realm name provided for your attendee ID. Enable the realm and
  save it.
. Switch to your new realm. Confirm its URL ends in `/realms/{realm_name}`.
. Create two users with non-shared lab credentials: one named `reader` and one
  named `manager`. Use synthetic email addresses `reader@example.invalid` and
  `manager@example.invalid`; these addresses do not require SMTP delivery.
. For each user, set **Email verified** to on before saving. Confirm the control
  shows on after the change; do not assume that clicking it changed its state.
. Set non-temporary passwords and use the lab's approved private credential
  handling method.

Leave both users without realm-role assignments for now. In Module 3, create
the `user` and `manager` roles, make `manager` include `user`, then assign the
roles to these users. Keycloak cannot assign a role before it exists.

== Check

Confirm both accounts are enabled, each has the expected synthetic email and
**Email verified** is on, and realm administration works inside your realm.
The role check happens after the roles are created in Module 3.

**Expected:** your issuer is `{keycloak_origin}/realms/{realm_name}` and you
cannot administer another attendee's realm or change server-wide settings.

== If something is wrong

If **Create realm** is missing, stop and ask the facilitator to check your
delegated `create-realm` permission. If the realm name collides, do not rename
another attendee's realm; request your exact assigned name. Do not grant
yourself `admin` or add roles in the master realm.
