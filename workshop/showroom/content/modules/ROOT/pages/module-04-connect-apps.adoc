= Module 4: Connect the applications
:navtitle: 4. Connect apps

== Know

These applications were built before the lab. You will connect them by changing
runtime configuration in your own namespace. The SPA reads `/config.json`;
server-side components read environment values from ConfigMaps and Secrets.
No source edit or rebuild is needed for this activity.

== Do

. In OpenShift Console, select your namespace. Inspect `frontend-runtime-config`
  and confirm `issuer` is your exact realm issuer and `spaClientId` is `spa`.
. Confirm the SPA, BFF, and proxy routes match your session handoff.
. Update the BFF ConfigMap issuer to your realm issuer. Confirm the existing
  BFF client ID is `bff`; this project fixes that ID in the application and
  expects the matching Keycloak client rather than a runtime override.
. Update the proxy ConfigMap issuer and callback to your realm issuer and exact
  proxy route.
. Update Orders, Inventory, and Notifications ConfigMaps so their issuer is
  your realm issuer. Confirm internal service URLs use your namespace, not
  `apps.svc`.
. Replace the `BFF_CLIENT_SECRET` value in `bff-client`, the `client-secret`
  value in `proxy-secrets`, and the `client-secret` value in
  `orders-api-client` with the matching secrets generated in module 3. The
  `cookie-secret` and `redis-password` fields are reservation-generated; keep
  them unchanged. Use the private console form, never command-line arguments
  or shared logs.
. Restart the corresponding Deployments through the console and wait for all
  readiness checks to pass.

== Check

Reload `/config.json` from your SPA route. It should show the realm issuer and
SPA client ID, with no secrets. Sign in as the reader through SPA, BFF, and
proxy. Each route must return to its own approved callback.

**Expected:** login succeeds against your realm and each app stays within your
namespace. The client secret does not appear in browser configuration.

== If something is wrong

Check issuer character-for-character, callback scheme/path, client ID, and
Secret key names against the Deployment's `secretKeyRef`. A stale pod may need a
rollout restart after a ConfigMap change. A BFF or proxy route can return 503
before the realm and clients exist; after this module, verify the issuer and
pod readiness if it persists. If the pod reports `ImagePullBackOff`, pause:
this is a reservation image-pull setup issue, not a learner task.
