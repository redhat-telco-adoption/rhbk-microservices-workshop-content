Open each console in its own browser tab. The OpenShift Console cannot be shown
inside this guide's split view: its tab there opens the Console in a new
browser tab and leaves an error in the pane, which you can ignore. Open Dev
Spaces in its own browser tab too, with the link above: the OpenShift and
Keycloak sign-in pages refuse to be shown inside another page, so a sign-in
cannot complete in the guide's **Dev Spaces** tab. Module 3 shows how to start
your workspace.

The **Terminal** tab is a shell with `curl` and `jq` in your Showroom. It
cannot read anything in your OpenShift namespace. Module 1 uses it; later
modules use terminals in your Dev Spaces workspace, which can.

== Your lab users

Operations created two users in your realm. Sign in to the apps as them, never
with your own account.

[cols="1,1,2",options="header"]
|===
|User |Password |Roles
|`reader` |`{reader_password}` |`user`: may view orders
|`manager` |`{manager_password}` |`manager`, which includes `user`: may view and create orders
|===

If a value shows `not-assigned`, `namespace-not-assigned`, `realm-not-assigned`,
or an `example.invalid` address, stop and ask the facilitator. Do not guess an
endpoint or use another attendee's realm.

== What operations prepared

* **Your realm**, `{realm_name}`, with every client, role, scope, and service
  account the apps need. You can read all of it in the Keycloak administration
  console, but you cannot change it: your account has view rights only. When
  you need a change, you ask operations, as you would at work.
* **Your developer namespace**, `{attendee_namespace}`, with the SPA frontend,
  BFF, proxy (and its Redis session store), the Orders, Inventory, and
  Notifications APIs, and the `reports` app for Module 8. All of them except
  `reports` are already connected to your realm: their ConfigMaps hold your
  issuer, and their Secrets hold their client secrets.
* **Your Dev Spaces workspace**, with Java and Maven, Python, Node.js, and
  `oc`, and the exercise sources. It may build and roll out applications in
  your developer namespace only. Builds stay in your namespace; nothing you
  build reaches any other attendee or the images the lab ships with.
* **Cluster logging and distributed tracing.** Your applications send logs and
  traces automatically; you read them in the OpenShift Console under
  **Observe → Logs** and **Observe → Traces**. Both show only your own data:
  logs from your namespace, and traces in your own tenant, `{attendee_id}`.

== Stop conditions

Pause and contact the facilitator if your namespace is not the one assigned to
you, your realm belongs to someone else, an app address belongs to another
attendee, or the Keycloak administration console lets you change settings.
Do not try to fix shared cluster or Keycloak settings yourself.

== Ground rules

Work only in your realm `{realm_name}` and namespace `{attendee_namespace}`.
Never put passwords, client secrets, tokens, or cookies in chat, tickets, or
screenshots.
